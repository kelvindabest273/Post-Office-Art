This drawing is for the 2024 Canada Post strike, which started on November 15th, 2024 because no new agreement was made between Canada Post and the Canadian Union of Postal Workers (CUPW) before the expiry of the previous agreement. The strike was suspended on December 17th, 2024 by the Canada Industrial Relations Board (CIRB), but the outage affected us Canadians until mid-January of 2025. The Canada Industrial Relations Board (CIRB) directed CUPW members back to work under the same collective agreement until May 22nd, 2025. CUPW did do some strike activity, but not to the extend as the last time. Fast forward to February 2025, a new school semester! With a new semester comes new courses and new things to learn!
