# Post Office Art

A piece of art made using Python's turtle of a Canada Post post office.
Oh boy do I have a story to tell...
So, the story starts back on the 15th of November, 2024, when Canada Post postal workers went on strike because no new agreement was made between Canada Post and the Canadian Union of Postal Workers (CUPW) before the expiry of the previous agreement. The strike was suspended on December 17th, 2024 by the Canada Industrial but it still affected us Canadians until the middle of January. 

This drawing is for the 2024 Canada Post strike, which started on November 15th, 2024 because no new agreement was made between Canada Post and the Canadian Union of Postal Workers (CUPW) before the expiry of the previous agreement. The strike was suspended on December 17th, 2024 by the Canada Industrial Relations Board (CIRB), but the outage affected us Canadians until mid-January of 2025. The Canada Industrial Relations Board (CIRB) directed CUPW members back to work under the same collective agreement until May 22nd, 2025. CUPW did do some strike activity, but not to the extend as the last time. Fast forward to February 2025, a new school semester! With a new semester comes new courses and new things to learn! One of my courses were Intro to Computer Science, and one of the first things we did was turtle Python. (im tired of talking professionally so imma keep it casual now)

so we had a project, make a drawing in turtle python, and i thought about the strike. i too was affected by the strike since i ordered something in november and it came at the end of december. anyways, i chose to make a drawing of a post office. i got full marks on the project!
